Astronomical Precision in Western Astrology: Swiss Ephemeris Standard

6 min read • October 2026 • Converglyph Research
### 1. Astronomical Foundation Western Astrology relies upon geocentric ecliptic longitudes measured along the Tropical Zodiac, anchored at the Vernal Equinox ($0^\circ$ Aries). Unlike casual horoscope apps that approximate positions through average orbital velocities, Converglyph Research calculates coordinates using `pyswisseph` (Swiss Ephemeris v2.10.3.2), derived from NASA JPL DE431 ephemerides. ### 2. Time Standards & Local Mean Time (LMT) Prior to international standard time zones (established between 1884 and 1920), local clocks operated on Local Mean Time (LMT), where noon corresponded to the Sun's meridian transit at that specific longitude. Converglyph Research explicitly calculates LMT offsets ($LMT = \ ext{Longitude} / 15.0$ hours) for historical benchmarks such as Albert Einstein (1879, Ulm, Germany: $\ ext{UTC}+0\ ext{h}39\ ext{m}57\ ext{s}$). ### 3. House Cusps & Aspect Geometry House divisions are calculated using Placidus semi-arc division, with Whole Sign secondary cross-checking. Aspect angles (Conjunction $0^\circ$, Sextile $60^\circ$, Square $90^\circ$, Trine $120^\circ$, Opposition $180^\circ$) are evaluated against classical strict orbs ($6^\circ - 8^\circ$), eliminating false angular claims.
