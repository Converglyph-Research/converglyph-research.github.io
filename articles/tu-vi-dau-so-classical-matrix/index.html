Tử Vi Đẩu Số: 12 Palaces, Star Brightness & Major Luck Cycles

8 min read • October 2026 • Converglyph Research
### 1. Lunisolar Mechanics Tử Vi Đẩu Số (Zi Wei Dou Shu) operates on the authentic Lunisolar Calendar, where months are measured from astronomical New Moon to New Moon. Converglyph implements the Ho Ngoc Duc calendar algorithm, which calculates lunar phases via Jean Meeus ephemeris equations to accurately resolve leap months (*tháng nhuận*). ### 2. Palace Assignment Matrix The 12 Palaces (*Thập Nhị Cung*) represent life domains: Life (*Mệnh*), Body (*Thân*), Career (*Quan Lộc*), Wealth (*Tài Bạch*), Marriage (*Phu Thê*), Ancestral Mind (*Phúc Đức*), and Assets (*Điền Trạch*). Life and Body palaces are established by intersecting the lunar birth month with the two-hour birth branch (*Chi giờ*). ### 3. Star Distribution & Transformation Converglyph establishes the Bureau (*Cục*) through the interaction of the Life Palace branch with the Year Stem. Once the Bureau number (2, 3, 4, 5, or 6) is established, the Emperor Star (*Tử Vi*) and Treasury Star (*Thiên Phủ*) are mapped deterministically, followed by the 4 Transformation Stars (*Tứ Hóa*: Khoa, Quyền, Lộc, Kỵ).
