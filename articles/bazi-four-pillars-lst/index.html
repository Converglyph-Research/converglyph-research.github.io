Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi): Solar Terms & True Solar Time

7 min read • October 2026 • Aetheria Research
### 1. The Solar Principle in BaZi While popular culture often conflates Chinese metaphysics with the Lunar New Year, authentic Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi / Tứ Trụ) is strictly a solar calendar system based on the Sun's apparent longitude along the ecliptic. The year transition occurs precisely at the onset of *Lichun* (Start of Spring, $315^\circ$ solar longitude), not the Lunar New Year. ### 2. True Solar Time ($LST$) Adjustment Because mean clock time assumes a uniform 24-hour day across broad longitudinal zones, BaZi hour pillars require two corrections: 1. **Longitudinal Offset:** $4 \text{ minutes per degree}$ from the standard meridian. 2. **Equation of Time ($EoT$):** Earth's orbital eccentricity and axial tilt shift true solar noon by up to $\pm 16 \text{ minutes}$ across the seasons. ### 3.
