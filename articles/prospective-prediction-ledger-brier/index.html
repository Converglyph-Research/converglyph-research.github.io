The Empirical Evaluation Framework: SHA-256 Ledger & Brier Loss

7 min read • October 2026 • Aetheria Research
### 1. Eliminating Post-Hoc Rationalization The primary flaw in conventional astrological testing is hindsight bias: interpretations are retrofitted after events become public knowledge. Aetheria Research enforces prospective locking: predictions must be serialized and locked with a full 64-hex SHA-256 cryptographic digest before the observation window commences. ### 2. The Brier Scoring Rule Probabilistic predictions are evaluated using the Brier score: $$Brier = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{t=1}^N (f_t - o_t)^2$$ Where $f_t \in [0, 1]$ is the forecast probability and $o_t \in \{0, 1\}$ is the binary observed outcome. Predictions that claim high certainty but fail are penalized quadratically. ### 3. Benchmarking Against the Null A forecasting system only possesses predictive resolution if its Brier score improves upon uncalibrated random coin-flip nulls ($0.250$) and historical base-rate baselines.
