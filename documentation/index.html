Documentation & Engine Specifications

Comprehensive reference covering calculation engines, astronomical assumptions, calendar boundary algorithms, formulas, and REST API endpoints.

1. Western Ephemeris Engine (`pyswisseph`)

Calculates high-precision geocentric planetary coordinates using the Swiss Ephemeris (standard Moshier and JPL DE431 integration).

Standard: Tropical Zodiac (Geocentric Ecliptic Longitude)
House System: Placidus Semi-Arc Division (with Whole Sign cross-check)
Aspect Orbs: Conjunction, Opposition, Trine: 8.0° | Square: 7.0° | Sextile: 6.0°
Timezone Handling: Historical Local Mean Time (LMT = Longitude / 15.0 hours)

2. Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi / Tứ Trụ)

Calculates the 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches based on astronomical 24 Solar Terms (Jie Qi).

Year Boundary: Lichun (Start of Spring, 315° solar longitude), not Lunar New Year
Hour Pillar Correction: True Solar Time (LST) adjusting for longitude and Equation of Time (EoT)
Day Pillar: Modulo-60 sexagenary day progression derived from astronomical Julian Day count

3. Tử Vi Đẩu Số Classical 12-Palace Matrix

Authentic lunisolar palace chart and star assignment based on the Ho Ngoc Duc calendar algorithms and classical Song Dynasty texts.

Lunisolar Resolution: Jean Meeus astronomical new moon calculation resolving leap months
Palace Establishment: Life Palace (Mệnh) = (Month Branch - Hour Branch + 12) % 12
Star Systems: 14 Major Stars, Auxiliary Stars, 4 Transformation Stars (Khoa, Quyền, Lộc, Kỵ)
