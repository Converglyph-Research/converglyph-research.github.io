Trust, Limitations & Open Science

We build tools to investigate what traditions say, how they differ, and what the evidence supports.

• Computed Facts vs. Interpretations: Astronomical planetary degrees and calendar conversions are calculated deterministically via Swiss Ephemeris and solar term algorithms.

• Empirical Testing under Peer Review: We do not assume symbolic systems can predict the future. We maintain a pre-registered forecast ledger to test claims against objective outcomes over time.

• Privacy by Design: We never ask for invasive device tracking, messages, or calendar access. Your personal data remains yours.
